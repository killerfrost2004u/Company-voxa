# VOXA: 3-Month SEO & Growth Plan

## Operational Mandate
This 90-day plan is built upon the Antigravity SEO Directives, prioritizing the transition from technical perfection (Month 1) to content velocity and algorithmic authority (Months 2 & 3). The objective is to dominate commercial intent queries and capture high-value enterprise leads.

---

## Month 1: Foundation & Technical Dominance
*(See `month_1_seo_plan.md` for daily breakdown)*
**Focus:** Perfecting the crawling, rendering, and indexing architecture.
*   **Technical SEO:** Submit sitemaps, strictly configure `robots.txt`, and ensure Next.js SSR performance.
*   **Schema & Semantic Markup:** Inject `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, and `Article` JSON-LD schemas.
*   **On-Page & Social:** Optimize core metadata, establish strict `<h1>`/`<h2>` hierarchies, and validate Open Graph cards.
*   **Result:** 100% Technical Indexation and branded search dominance.

---

## Month 2: Content Velocity & E-E-A-T Scaling
**Focus:** Deploying targeted informational and commercial content to satisfy specific user search intents.

*   **Week 1: Informational Intent Capture (Top of Funnel)**
    *   **Action:** Publish 4 long-form technical articles targeting informational queries (e.g., "Benefits of Next.js for Enterprise e-commerce").
    *   **Action:** Enforce strict E-E-A-T standards: Every article must have an author bio, publish dates, and external citations to authoritative tech sources (like Vercel or AWS).
*   **Week 2: Commercial Intent Optimization (Middle of Funnel)**
    *   **Action:** Expand the `services` and `work` case studies. Use keyword-rich, outcome-focused headings (e.g., "Scaling E-Commerce Conversions by 300%").
    *   **Action:** Embed internal links from the new informational articles directly to these commercial case studies.
*   **Week 3: ASO Preparation (If Applicable)**
    *   **Action:** If VOXA plans to launch client apps, draft optimized iOS App Store titles (Brand + Core Feature) and Google Play Long Descriptions (targeting 2-3% keyword density).
*   **Week 4: Performance & CTR Auditing**
    *   **Action:** Review Google Search Console. Identify pages ranking on Page 2 or 3 of Google. Rewrite their Meta Descriptions to act as high-conversion ad copy to improve Click-Through Rate (CTR).

---

## Month 3: Authority Building & Algorithmic Trust
**Focus:** Proving to search engines that VOXA is the premier authority in the software engineering space through external signals and deep semantic relevance.

*   **Week 1-2: Advanced Schema & Rich Snippets**
    *   **Action:** Implement `SoftwareApplication` or `Product` schema on specific case studies or proprietary tools.
    *   **Action:** Add `FAQPage` schema to the contact and services pages to aggressively target Google's "People Also Ask" drop-downs.
*   **Week 3: Internal Link Graph Fortification**
    *   **Action:** Conduct an orphaned-page audit. Ensure every article and case study has at least 3 internal links pointing to it from other high-authority pages on the site, distributing "link juice" evenly.
*   **Week 4: The 90-Day Evaluation**
    *   **Action:** Analyze 90-day search query data. Double down on content clusters (e.g., AI Integration vs. Web Dev) that are showing the highest impression growth.

---

## Expected Results (End of Month 3)

By the end of 90 days, the compounding effects of technical perfection and content velocity will yield measurable business outcomes:

1.  **Page 1 Rankings for Long-Tail Commercial Queries:** VOXA will rank on the first page of Google for highly specific, high-intent queries (e.g., "Custom AI enterprise solutions agency" or "Next.js migration agency").
2.  **Organic Traffic Growth (300%+ increase):** The steady publishing of E-E-A-T optimized articles will drive a consistent, compounding stream of Informational search traffic.
3.  **Rich Snippet Ownership:** VOXA will appear in Google's "People Also Ask" sections and display Rich Results (star ratings, FAQ drop-downs, article thumbnails) directly in the search results, dramatically increasing CTR.
4.  **Inbound Lead Generation:** Because the commercial intent pages are strictly optimized for conversions (and linked from high-traffic articles), organic traffic will begin converting into actual "Schedule a Consultation" leads via the Contact page.
5.  **Algorithmic Trust:** Search engines will semantically associate the "VOXA" entity with "High-Performance Software Engineering," making future content index and rank significantly faster than it did in Month 1.
