# Service & Package Page Architecture (VOXA)

VOXA offers premium software and marketing services (ATS, E-learning, Cyber Security, Branding). Our service pages must be structured to educate the client and drive conversions.

## 1. Standard Page Layout
Every service page MUST include:
1. **Hero Section:** Clear value proposition, premium typography, and a primary CTA ("Start Project").
2. **Visual Anchor:** Incorporate the specific SVG assets from `public/assets/svgs/` (e.g., `Voxa(ATS)design1.svg`) prominently in the hero or feature sections to illustrate the service.
3. **Problem / Solution Statement:** Why does the client need this? What pain point does it solve?
4. **Features Breakdown:** Use alternating layouts (text on left, image/SVG on right, then flip) to explain the technical capabilities (using `lucide-react` icons for bullet points).
5. **Pricing / Packages:** If applicable (as per the "باقات تصميم وتطوير المواقع" doc), display clean, tiered pricing tables. Highlight the "Recommended" tier.
6. **Final CTA & Lead Form:** Always end the page with a lead generation form (refer to the `lead-generation-forms` skill).

## 2. Content & Tone
- The tone is professional, premium, and authoritative, written in Arabic.
- Focus on ROI (Return on Investment) for the client.
- Ensure all copy flows naturally from Right-to-Left (RTL).
